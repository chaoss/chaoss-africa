# CHAOSS Africa Designers Focus Group

This subgroup supports contributors interested in UI/UX, visual communication, design systems, and creative assets for CHAOSS Africa programs.

## How to join
1. Introduce yourself in `#chaoss-africa` on Slack and mention you want to join the Designers group.
2. Review existing design tasks and ask for context before starting.
3. Share drafts early for feedback and iteration.

## Suggested first tasks
- Propose one design improvement for a community-facing document/page.
- Improve readability/consistency of one existing visual asset.
- Support a maintainer with a small issue labeled for design contributions.
