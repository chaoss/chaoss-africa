# CHAOSS Africa Researchers Focus Group

## About
This subgroup supports contributors interested in open source community research, metrics analysis, and evidence-based community insights.

## What this group works on
- Researchers onboarding document: [Read here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1uAWrbeKoV82xrgVCkh-67VE4PYh9BsW4Y6gRFVkhnAo/edit#heading=h.ig1n5k2k62sv)
- Ongoing research tracker: [Work tracking doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1qpp2SSPS9eo5X_LTsmclG5O4nLpxqRwOagmgiOF2hPQ/edit)
- Research synthesis and reporting support

## Meeting time

## How to join
1. Introduce yourself in `#chaoss-africa` on Slack and mention you want to join the Researchers group.
2. Review the onboarding and tracker documents.
3. Pick one scoped research/documentation item and coordinate with leads.

## Suggested first tasks
- Help clean up or structure an existing research note.
- Validate links and references in research documents.
- Summarize one current research item into actionable bullets.
