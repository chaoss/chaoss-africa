# CHAOSS Africa Community Managers Focus Group

## About
This subgroup supports contributors focused on community engagement, contributor experience, communication, and ecosystem growth.

## What this group works on
- Community onboarding and participation support
- Community programming and communications
- Contributor engagement activities

## Meeting time
- Meeting schedule: **To be announced in `#chaoss-africa` Slack channel**

## Group lead
- **Ruth Ikegah**

## How to join
1. Introduce yourself in `#chaoss-africa` on Slack and mention you want to join the Community Managers group.
2. Ask for current activities where volunteer support is needed.
3. Start with one scoped task and share regular progress updates.

## Suggested first tasks
- Help refine onboarding or participation messaging in docs.
- Support issue triage and contributor follow-ups.
- Draft a short post/update for a community activity.
